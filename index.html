About

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Zurich in the group of Prof. Torsten Hothorn. My research combines statistical methodology and machine learning to draw reliable, interpretable conclusions from complex biomedical data. I focus on who benefits from a treatment and how to estimate such effects transparently.

I received my PhD in Statistics from LMU Munich, supervised by Prof. Bernd Bischl. Afterwards, I served as interim professor in the Elite Graduate Program in Data Science at LMU. My work spans heterogeneous treatment effect estimation, survival analysis, interpretable and fair machine learning, and open-source research software in R.

  • Causal inference
  • Heterogeneous treatment effects
  • Survival analysis
  • Transformation models
  • Interpretable machine learning
  • Algorithmic fairness
